# Targeted Conferences and Journals for EpilepsyGuard

Based on the project's focus on **Real-Time Seizure Prediction**, **IoT**, **Machine Learning**, and **Big Data (Flink/Kafka)**, the following venues are recommended for submission:

## Conferences

### 1. AI Epilepsy 2026
- **Full Name:** 4th International Artificial Intelligence in Epilepsy and Neurological Disorders Conference
- **Dates:** March 16-19, 2026
- **Location:** San Juan, Puerto Rico
- **Relevance:** Highly specific to this project. Focuses on clinically deployed AI in neuroscience.
- **Deadline:** Abstract submission January 29, 2026 (Note: This is very soon!).

### 2. IEEE EMBC 2026
- **Full Name:** 48th Annual International Conference of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society
- **Relevance:** The flagship conference for biomedical engineering.
- **Focus:** Digital health, biomedical signal processing.

### 3. IEEE CHASE 2026
- **Full Name:** IEEE/ACM Conference on Connected Health: Applications, Systems and Engineering Technologies
- **Relevance:** Perfect for IoT-based healthcare applications.
- **Focus:** Real-time monitoring, security in healthcare.

### 4. BHI 2026
- **Full Name:** IEEE International Conference on Biomedical and Health Informatics
- **Relevance:** Focuses on informatics in healthcare, AI/ML applications.

## Journals

### 1. IEEE Access

- **Type:** Open Access.

### 2. Epilepsia Open
- **Impact Factor:** 2.9
- **Relevance:** Official journal of the ILAE focusing on innovative research.
- **Focus:** Clinical and basic science epilepsy research.

### 3. Seizure: European Journal of Epilepsy
- **Impact Factor:** ~3.4
- **Relevance:** High clinical impact.

### 4. JMIR (Journal of Medical Internet Research)
- **Relevance:** Focuses on digital health and IoT.
